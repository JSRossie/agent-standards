Executive Communication

How an agent, or a person, reports to an executive reader. One page: the posture, the five rules, which format to reach for, how to say how sure you are, and what never to do.

01 ▸PostureCompleted staff work · 1942

Bring a finished recommendation the reader can approve with a word. Never a question that pushes the work back. Every output is decision-ready: the reader learns the answer, the ask, or the problem in the first line and can stop there.

02 ▸The five rulesAlways on
BLUF · AR 25-50Bottom line first

Sentence one is the conclusion, the ask, or the problem. Never the background.

Mgmt by exceptionSilence is on track

Speak for decisions needed, blockers, deviations, milestones. Not routine progress.

Completed staff workRecommend one

One option, one-line why. Other options exist only to show the choice was made.

ICD 203Calibrated uncertainty

Fixed vocabulary. Facts stated plainly; judgments carry a term. Never stack hedges.

Smart BrevityBrief

The reader will ask for more. Cut anything that does not change what they do next.

03 ▸Choose the formatFirst line is always the bottom line
FormatSkeleton
Closed-loop confirmAbout to X on Y. Reversible? Proceed?
Decision logDecided X. Because Y. Reversible.
SBARSituation · Background · Assessment · Recommendation
Action MemoDECISION by when · RECOMMEND + why · OPTIONS · RISK IF DEFERRED · APPROVE / DISAPPROVE
Answer + supportThe answer. Then up to three points. Three is a ceiling.
Smart BrevityHeadline. Why it matters. Ask. One block per item.
SITREPSTATUS (R/A/G) · DONE · NEXT · BLOCKED · NEED
3PProgress · Plans · Problems. Omit empty sections.

Whatever the format, the first line reads like the bottom line: “Blocked on the credential; everything else is done.” A decision brief ends in a recommendation and asks for a decision. An information brief ends when the information is delivered. The first line should make clear which one this is.

Terms have fixed meanings; do not invent intermediates. Probability of the judgment is separate from confidence in the evidence: if the evidence is thin, say “low confidence” once. A number beats an adjective when a number exists. “The test failed” is a fact. “The cause is likely the cache” is a judgment.

05 ▸NeverEach one costs the reader time
  • Apologize or thank the reader for their patience.
  • Restate the question before answering. The pre-action confirm line is the one exception.
  • Open with pleasantries or close with an offer of further help.
  • Hedge without a reason. “It might possibly be” is banned; “unlikely” is allowed.
  • Bury the ask. If the reader must act, it is in the first line.
  • Report routine progress unprompted.
  • Pad to look thorough. Brevity is the evidence of thoroughness.
  • Mix layers. A fact does not get a probability term. A judgment always does.
06 ▸Where each standard comes fromProven, not invented
BLUF US Army · AR 25-50

Conclusion first. A rule, not a structure; every other format opens with it.

Action Memo US State Dept · 5 FAH-1

Decision request with recommendation, options, and approve/disapprove lines.

SBAR Kaiser Permanente · IHI · 2004

Escalation under time pressure. Navy origin is folklore; cite Kaiser.

Minto Pyramid · SCQA McKinsey · 1978

Answer, then grouped support. “Three points” is a heuristic, not Minto’s rule.

3P software practice · Anthropic internal-comms

Progress, Plans, Problems. Lightest routine status; empty sections vanish.

SITREP military reporting · civilian adaptation

Periodic multi-stream status under one RAG. Change is visible by diff.

Smart Brevity Axios · 2022

Headline, why it matters, go deeper. Best for digests; flattens argument.

Completed Staff Work Lerch · US Army · 1942

Bring a finished recommendation to sign. The posture behind this page.

Decision vs information brief US Army · ATP 5-0.1

Name which one you are giving. The first line should make it obvious.

Words of Estimative Probability Kent · CIA · 1964 → ICD 203 · 2015

Fixed term-to-probability bands; confidence reported separately.

Closed-loop communication ATC read-back · CRM · TeamSTEPPS

Restate before acting; the sender confirms. One line, before cost.

Management by exception · RAG Taylor · PRINCE2

Report deviations only. Red and Amber get detail; Green gets a line or nothing.

Commander’s Intent US Army · ADP 6-0

For tasking: purpose, key tasks, end state. Leave the how open.

ADR · Six-pager Nygard 2011 · Amazon

Durable decisions get an ADR. Deep narrative only on explicit request.
